Agentic AI IntroductionA governed AI layer above your existing systems

A governed AI operations layer above the existing AVEVA SCADA and CMMS environment — aggregating context and reasoning, not replacing systems of record.

How it works
1
Connect

Alarm, process and event data aggregated from the SCADA environment — no change to source systems.

2
Contextualize

The Semantic Data Platform models assets, alarms and locations as relationships, not flat tags.

3
Reason

Governed AI evaluates severity, location and precedent to prioritize and draft reports.

4
Act

Approved tickets raised in the existing CMMS; shift reports and dashboards issued.

Core components
Siemens Semantic Data Platform

The common information model serving the AI layer — establishing relationships between all factory entities so AI agents reason with context, not raw tags.

Governed AI Platform Layer

Workflow orchestration and AI integration with human review built in — the layer where AI agents carry out operational tasks in parallel.

Click Connect, Contextualize, Reason or Act above to see it animate here
Connect — one layer across your systems

SCADA, CMMS, LIMS, Historian and ERP connect through standard interfaces — read-only where needed, no changes to the source systems.

Contextualize — drag any node to explore

A labeled property graph — assets, alarms, locations, work orders and documents linked as typed relationships. Drag any node.

Reason — neural graph reasoning trace

Governed AI propagates signal across the knowledge graph — severity, location and precedent converge into a scored root cause.

Act — approve, then act

Approved tickets raised in the existing CMMS; shift reports and dashboards issued — nothing changes until a person approves.
